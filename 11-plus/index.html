11+ & Entrance

Teach the thinking behind the answer.

Entrance preparation should develop the habits pupils need when the text, question or problem is unfamiliar, not just rehearse one paper format.

Comprehension

Inference with evidence

Learn how to locate clues, explain what they imply and avoid vague or unsupported answers.

Writing

Control, clarity and effect

Build sentences, vocabulary, structure and editing habits that make writing deliberate rather than decorated.

Reasoning

Methods that transfer

Practise patterns and problem types while keeping the emphasis on explaining how the answer was reached.

Preparation without spoon-feeding.

Strong pupils still need challenge. The goal is to give enough modelling to reveal a better method, then remove support so the pupil can reproduce it independently.

A
Unfamiliar texts

Build confidence with vocabulary, tone, inference and writer’s choices.

B
Timed thinking

Improve pace without sacrificing accuracy or explanation.

C
Interview-style discussion

Practise explaining ideas aloud when appropriate to the target school.

Tell me the schools, test format and current strengths.

The preparation can then focus on the areas that genuinely need work.
