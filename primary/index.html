Primary Tuition

Fix small gaps before they become big ones.

Primary tuition is most useful when it strengthens understanding, confidence and independence rather than simply adding more schoolwork.

Clear foundations in English and Maths.

Lessons can support a pupil who is falling behind, stretch a pupil who needs more challenge, or rebuild confidence after a difficult topic.

  • Reading comprehension and inference
  • Vocabulary, spelling, grammar and sentence control
  • Creative and non-fiction writing
  • Number, fractions, decimals and percentages
  • Reasoning and multi-step problem solving

Age-appropriate, not watered down

Explanations are made accessible without removing the thinking. Pupils are encouraged to explain their choices, spot their own errors and gradually rely less on prompting.

Explain

Use concrete examples, diagrams and carefully chosen questions to make an abstract idea understandable.

Practise

Repeat the skill in slightly different forms so the pupil learns the method rather than one answer.

Remember

Return to important knowledge later so it becomes secure enough to use independently.

Not sure exactly where the gap is?

That is fine. Send the year group, subject and a short description of what you have noticed.
