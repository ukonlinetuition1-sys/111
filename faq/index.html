FAQ

Straight answers before you enquire.

If your question is specific to a pupil, send a short enquiry rather than trying to fit them into a generic category.

Who is tuition for?

Pupils who need clearer explanations, more challenge, more confidence or more precise feedback. Support is available across Primary, 11+/entrance preparation and GCSE.

Are lessons 1:1?

1:1 lessons are available, alongside small-group tuition where there is a suitable match of age, subject and level.

What happens in the first lesson?

The first lesson combines diagnosis and teaching. Existing work or school feedback can be used to identify priorities, but the pupil should still leave having learned something useful.

Can handwritten work be used online?

Yes. Scans or clear photographs of handwritten work can be useful because they show the pupil’s real process, including crossings-out, working and recurring errors.

Do you set homework?

Homework can be set where it is useful. It should reinforce a specific lesson objective rather than add work for the sake of it.

Which GCSE exam boards can you support?

Teaching can be aligned to the pupil’s actual specification and paper format. The exact board and texts should be included in the enquiry where known.

How do online lessons work?

Lessons use live video alongside shared documents, questions, annotated work and digital resources. The structure is interactive rather than a recorded lecture.

How quickly should progress happen?

That depends on the starting point, frequency of lessons and the type of difficulty. Early progress is often visible first in the quality of the pupil’s decisions before it appears in a formal grade.

Still unsure whether the fit is right?

Describe the pupil and the current problem. You do not need to commit to anything to ask.
