GCSE Tuition

Build the knowledge and exam habits that actually move the grade.

GCSE support is focused on the specific knowledge, writing decisions and problem-solving habits that are limiting performance, rather than simply doing more papers.

English Language

Make answers more precise

Inference, analysis, comparison, evaluation, structure and transactional or creative writing taught through explicit models and repeated practice.

English Literature

Turn knowledge into argument

Secure text knowledge, quotations and context, then learn how to shape them into a focused response to the exact question.

Maths

Find the gap behind the error

Targeted support for missing methods, weak fluency and multi-step problems, with worked examples followed by increasingly independent practice.

Not every lost mark has the same cause.

Two pupils can receive the same mark for completely different reasons. One may lack knowledge; another may understand the content but misread the task, write too vaguely or run out of time.

  • Separate knowledge gaps from exam-technique problems.
  • Model high-quality thinking before expecting independent performance.
  • Use mark schemes as evidence, not as scripts to memorise.
  • Revisit recurring weaknesses until they become reliable strengths.

Boards and materials

Teaching can be aligned to the pupil’s actual exam board and school course. Existing mocks, assessments and teacher feedback are useful starting points because they reveal patterns that generic revision resources often miss.
