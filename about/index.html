About

Teaching experience matters most when it changes what happens in the lesson.

UK Online Tuition is built around diagnostic teaching: notice what the pupil is doing, identify the highest-value next step and teach it clearly.

DH

Daniel Harris

Founder & tutor

13+years in education
PGCEqualified teacher

From classroom leadership to focused tuition.

Daniel has worked as a Head of English, Assistant Head of Sixth Form and KS3 Coordinator. That background brings together subject teaching, curriculum design, assessment and pastoral understanding.

Online tuition makes it possible to apply that experience with unusual precision: one pupil, one screen, one set of misconceptions and one next step at a time.

✓

✓
Challenge without overwhelming

Tasks should sit just beyond what the pupil can currently do alone.

✓
Use feedback immediately

The next attempt should be better because the feedback changed the pupil’s decision-making.

What the service is trying to avoid.

Good tuition is not a second school lesson delivered more quietly.

Generic revision

Covering a topic because it is “next” even when the pupil’s real weakness lies elsewhere.

Answer dependency

Giving so much help that the pupil succeeds only while the tutor is present.

Vague praise

“Good work” is less useful than identifying the exact choice that improved the answer.
