Resources

Short guidance that changes what you do next.

The best resource is one that makes the next attempt better. These topics reflect common problems that appear repeatedly in tuition.

GCSE English

From feature spotting to analysis

Ask three questions: what choice has the writer made, what does it make us think or feel, and why does that matter here?

Comprehension

Inference without guessing

Underline the clue first. Then state what it suggests and explain the connection between the evidence and the inference.

Writing

Improve a sentence deliberately

Change one variable at a time: verb choice, sentence length, image, punctuation or viewpoint. Improvement is easier to judge when the purpose is clear.

Maths

When “careless mistakes” keep happening

Repeated mistakes often have a pattern: skipped working, weak recall, notation errors or misreading. Name the pattern before trying to fix it.

Revision

Practise retrieval, not recognition

Looking at notes can feel fluent because the answer is visible. Close the notes, produce the idea from memory, then check it.

Parents

What useful homework feedback sounds like

Ask what the pupil changed after feedback and whether they could now do the same thing without the tutor’s prompt.

Need guidance on a specific piece of work?

A school essay, mock paper or problem set can be a better starting point than a generic revision list.
